Weekly plan

Each week: learn the objectives, do one hands-on lab, then a quiz of 20-40 questions on that week's material. Check items off as you finish them.

Readiness rule of thumb used here: three consecutive full-length practice exams at 85% or higher before sitting the exam. This is a study target, not CompTIA's scoring scale.

Method

  • Study time follows the domain weights: Security Operations (28%) and Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Mitigations (22%) get the most days.
  • 60-day path: about 10-12 hours per week. 90-day path: about 6-8 hours per week, plus a dedicated remediation week.
  • Read the verb in each objective. "Given a scenario" objectives (2.4, 3.2, 4.1, 4.5, 4.6, 4.9, 5.6) are likely PBQ material, so practice them hands-on, not only with flashcards.
  • Build an acronym deck from the acronym list in the objectives PDF and review it daily for 10 minutes.
  • Do the PBQs last on exam day if they stall you. Flag and come back.
  • Labs: a home lab (Proxmox or VirtualBox) running Kali, a Windows VM, and a Linux server covers most practical objectives. Wireshark, nmap, firewall rules, Group Policy, log review, and certificate generation with OpenSSL are all good practice.
