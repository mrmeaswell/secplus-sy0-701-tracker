# CompTIA Certification Study Tracker

Interactive 60- or 90-day study pathways mapped to the official CompTIA exam objectives. Pick a certification at the top of the page:

| Track | Exam |
|---|---|
| Security+ | SY0-701 |
| A+ Core 1 | 220-1201 |
| Cloud+ | CV0-004 |
| Network+ | N10-009 |
| Server+ | SK0-005 |
| Linux+ | XK0-006 (draft objectives v1.0) |

**Open the tracker:** https://mrmeaswell.github.io/secplus-sy0-701-tracker/

## How to use
1. Choose a certification, the 60- or 90-day pathway, and your start date.
2. Work through each week's study tasks, lab, and quiz, and check them off.
3. Rate your confidence (1-4) on each objective.
4. Log practice exam scores. Aim for three full practice exams at 85%+ before booking the exam.

Each track keeps its own progress in your browser. Use **Export all** to back it up and **Import** to restore it on another device.

Study weeks are assigned by domain weight. Objective titles and topics come from CompTIA's published exam objectives documents; see the official PDFs for every sub-item. Not affiliated with or endorsed by CompTIA.
